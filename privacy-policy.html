Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026 · Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Blue Palm Investment Group LLC, doing business as Blue Palm Fast Cash Home Buyers (“Blue Palm,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit bluepalmig.com, submit a form, call us, or exchange text messages with us.

Blue Palm Investment Group LLC · 942 Harpersville Rd, Newport News, VA 23601 · (757) 870-7033 · info@bluepalmig.com

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, property address, phone number, email address, and anything you tell us about your property or your situation through a form, a call, a text message, or email.
  • Information collected automatically. IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring page, and general location, collected through cookies and analytics tools.
  • Public and third-party records. When you ask us for an offer, we may review publicly available property records (assessor, deed, and court records) and data from service providers to evaluate the property.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and prepare and present a cash offer on your property.
  • To contact you by phone, text message, and email about your inquiry and your property.
  • To send you marketing communications, but only if you separately opted in to receive them.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website.
  • To meet legal, tax, and record-keeping obligations.

3. Text messaging (SMS) program

By checking the consent box on any form on this site, or by texting us first, you agree to receive text messages from Blue Palm Fast Cash Home Buyers at the mobile number you provided. Consent is not a condition of any purchase, sale, or service, and marketing messages require a separate opt-in from transactional messages.

  • Message types. Transactional and conversational messages about your inquiry (property details, questions, responses, offer follow-up, and appointment confirmations), and, with separate consent, marketing messages about offers and services.
  • Message frequency. Message frequency varies based on your conversation with us.
  • Cost. Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier is not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Opt out. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving text messages. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages.
  • Help. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at (757) 870-7033 or info@bluepalmig.com.
  • Supported carriers. Carriers in the United States, including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, and their affiliates and resellers.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors that provide support services, such as customer service and message delivery, is permitted. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not sell or rent your phone number.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working and to understand how it is used. Our site currently uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, which collect usage data such as pages visited, clicks, and session activity. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

5. When we share information

We share personal information only as described here:

  • Service providers who work on our behalf, such as website hosting, form processing, customer relationship software, phone and messaging providers, and analytics. They may use the information only to perform services for us.
  • Transaction partners such as title companies, attorneys, inspectors, and lenders, when they are needed to complete a transaction you have asked us to pursue.
  • Legal reasons, when required by law, subpoena, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfer, if our business or its assets are sold or reorganized.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we never share SMS opt-in data or consent with anyone for their own marketing.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, complete a transaction, and meet legal, tax, and record-keeping requirements, and then delete or anonymize it. Records of your SMS consent and opt-out requests are kept for as long as required by applicable law.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop text messages.
  • Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or reply asking to be removed.
  • Ask us not to call you, and we will add you to our internal do-not-call list.
  • Ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

Virginia residents have rights under the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act, including the right to confirm, access, correct, delete, and obtain a copy of their personal data, and to appeal a decision we make about a request. To exercise any of these rights, email info@bluepalmig.com or call (757) 870-7033. We will verify your identity before acting on a request and will respond within the time the law allows.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Links to other sites

Our site may link to websites we do not control. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those sites, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed, and the updated version applies as soon as it is posted.
